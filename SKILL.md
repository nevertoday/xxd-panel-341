## Panel-specific overlay

The two halves are one continuous event, not a photo above a separate drawing. Find the single visual opportunity already in the photograph — a direction, an edge, a pose, a line, a force — and let only that thing cross the midline into warm off-white paper, where it is continued, repurposed, completed or reversed. Any small black line-drawn figures must be doing the event, never decorating it. The source brief asks for one short handwritten English aside; when the user picks no text language, keep it English. Reject a second unrelated illustration below, a cartoon redraw of the photo, dense doodles, sticker-like props, a stock pun, and a motivational quote.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.
